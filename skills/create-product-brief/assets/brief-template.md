# Product Brief: <Product name>

## Executive Summary
2–3 paragraphs: what it is, the problem, why it matters, why now. Must stand alone.

## The Problem
Who feels the pain, how they cope today, the cost of the status quo. Real scenarios.

## The Solution
What is built and what experience/outcome it creates. No implementation.

## What Makes This Different
Honest differentiators. If the edge is execution speed, say so. Cite research digests (`research\landscape.md`).

## Who This Serves
Primary users, vivid but brief: who, what they need, what success looks like. Secondary users if relevant.

## Core User Flow
One paragraph: the most important thing the end user does, start to finish, in a concrete scene (a named person, a context). The PRD expands this into numbered journeys.

## Success Criteria
User success signals + business objectives, measurable. At least one counter-signal (what we must not optimise at the cost of).

## Scope
First version: in. Explicitly out. A boundary document, not a feature list.

## Open Questions
- Question — owner — revisit when

## Vision
Where this goes in 2–3 years if it succeeds. Grounded.
