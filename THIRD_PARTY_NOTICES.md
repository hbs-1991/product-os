## BMad Method

The discovery chain (`create-product-brief`, `create-prd`, `create-ux-design`, `create-architecture`, `research-protocol`) is adapted from the ideas, structure and workflow disciplines of the BMad Method skills `bmad-product-brief`, `bmad-prd`, `bmad-ux`, `bmad-architecture` and `bmad-deep-recon`, re-shaped for this plugin's pipeline. The `DESIGN.md` format follows the Google Labs DESIGN.md specification.
